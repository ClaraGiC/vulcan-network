13–15 November 2026 · Morella, Spain

VULCAN at the International Bearded Vulture Meeting

Frederick Verbruggen and Lucy Mitchell presented VULCAN at the International Bearded Vulture Meeting 2026 in Morella, organised by the Vulture Conservation Foundation, the Generalitat Valenciana and the town of Morella.

It was very nice to get to know the people behind the bearded vulture reintroduction programme and to hear how well the programme is developing. We heard very inspiring talks, met new potential partners and discussed new avenues for studying bearded vulture behaviour and cognition.

29–30 April 2025 · Ghent, Belgium

First VULCAN workshop in Ghent

On 29 and 30 April 2025, a small initial group of VULCAN partners spent two wonderful days at Monasterium PoortAckere in Ghent for the first VULCAN workshop.

Not all partners could be there, but the group brought together people working on tracking data, field experiments and conservation. It was exciting to decide together on the first research questions of the network, from how young vultures learn their landscape to how they use feeding sites. We also discussed how to expand the network to new partners, species and countries.

The questions agreed in Ghent now shape the first VULCAN projects.
