13–15 November 2026 · Morella, Spain

VULCAN at the International Bearded Vulture Meeting

Frederick Verbruggen and Lucy Mitchell presented VULCAN at the International Bearded Vulture Meeting 2026 in Morella. The meeting was organised by the Vulture Conservation Foundation, the Generalitat Valenciana and the town of Morella.

29–30 April 2025 · Ghent, Belgium

First VULCAN workshop in Ghent

Partners met in Ghent for the first VULCAN workshop. Together they set the first research questions of the network and agreed how partners share data and work together.
